A NEW WAY TO LEARN

Olive Tree – The Future School

Olive Tree – The Future School is dedicated to developing an educational environment that prepares children for academic success and the opportunities and responsibilities of an evolving world. Meaningful learning extends beyond textbooks and examinations: children need to explore, question, experiment, collaborate and apply knowledge to real-life situations.

From preschool through Grade 7, our proposed learning framework supports progressive academic development while nurturing creativity, discipline, confidence, emotional intelligence and ethical values.

Our Educational Philosophy

Education should empower children to think independently, act responsibly and connect classroom concepts with real-world experiences.

Through exploration, collaboration, practical activities and meaningful guidance, children can discover their strengths, develop confidence and build a foundation for lifelong learning.
