OUR LEARNING APPROACH

Learning with Purpose. Growing with Confidence.

Primary education is an important stage in which children strengthen their understanding of the world and develop essential academic and life skills. Our programme encourages curiosity, thoughtful questions, active participation and a positive attitude towards learning.

Lessons combine classroom instruction with interactive discussions, hands-on exercises, collaborative activities and opportunities to apply knowledge in everyday situations.

Alongside academic learning, children develop communication, creativity, digital awareness, teamwork, discipline and respect for others.

GROWING STEP BY STEP

Every Grade. A New Discovery.

Grade 1

Strengthening early reading, writing, number awareness, classroom routines and confidence through guided activities and exploration.

Grade 2

Developing fluency in basic literacy and mathematics while encouraging observation, expression, cooperation and independent effort.

Grade 3

Building comprehension, problem-solving, practical application and a broader understanding of the environment and society.

Grade 4

Encouraging deeper subject understanding, analytical thinking, collaborative learning and responsibility for learning tasks.

Grade 5

Consolidating essential academic skills, communication, self-management and readiness for the next stage of education.

OUR LEARNING AREAS

Knowledge for Everyday Life

English

Reading, writing, listening, speaking and vocabulary activities develop communication and language comprehension.

Mathematics

Numbers, patterns, measurements and problem-solving exercises build logical thinking and numerical confidence.

Environmental Studies and Science

Observation and hands-on exploration introduce nature, living things, the environment and scientific thinking.

Social Understanding

Learning about communities, relationships, culture and the world encourages awareness, respect and responsible citizenship.

Additional Languages

Languages offered by the school support vocabulary, listening, speaking and appreciation of different cultures.

Digital Awareness

Age-appropriate digital learning introduces technology awareness, responsible use and essential digital skills.

Art and Creativity

Drawing, painting, craft and creative projects nurture imagination, self-expression and appreciation of art.

Physical Education

Movement, games and physical activities encourage fitness, coordination, teamwork and an active lifestyle.

Values and Life Skills

Everyday learning encourages kindness, honesty, discipline, responsibility, cooperation and respect for others.
