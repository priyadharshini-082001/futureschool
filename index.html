Olive Tree – The Future School

About us

Olive Tree – The Future School is dedicated to developing an educational environment that prepares children for academic success and the opportunities and responsibilities of an evolving world. We strive to nurture curious minds, responsible individuals and confident learners who are ready to embrace the future. Meaningful learning extends beyond textbooks and examinations. Children need opportunities to explore, question, experiment, collaborate and apply knowledge to real-life situations. From preschool through Grade 7, our proposed learning framework supports progressive academic development while nurturing creativity, discipline, confidence, emotional intelligence and ethical values.

Our Academic Framework

Our academic framework supports continuous progression from early childhood to the middle-school years. Our CBSE-oriented approach emphasises conceptual clarity, competency development, experiential learning, analytical thinking and practical application.
